1 Hygiene design certificate

This certificate confirms that the TB-200 and TB-400 blister lines in the Pharma-GMP configuration comply with the requirements for hygiene design of packaging machines in pharmaceutical primary packaging that Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG has laid down in its hygiene design guideline. It applies jointly to both machine types because they use the same product-contact assemblies, the same materials and the same cleaning concept. In the myTannwald portal the certificate is assigned to the serial numbers of both types.

The certificate is provided in German and English; in case of discrepancies the German version applies. It supplements the certificate of conformity of the respective machine type and does not replace it. This certificate does not apply to the KT-500 cartoner because the cartoner has no product-contact parts within the meaning of primary packaging.

2 Safety

The hygiene design does not change the safety rules of the operating instructions. The safety chapter of the operating instructions TB-200, chapter 2, or of the operating instructions TB-400, chapter 2, applies to all cleaning work: the line is de-energised before wet cleaning, the protective hood of the sealing station is locked, hot parts are touched only after cooling down and only the approved cleaning agents are used. The ease of cleaning of the assemblies does not release the operator from these rules; an easily accessible sealing jaw is still hot and an easily removable hood is still a protective device.

4 Design principles

The hygiene design guideline lays down principles that are implemented in the design of the product-contact assemblies of both machine types. They are the basis for the cleaning intervals of the maintenance schedules and for the approval of the cleaning agents.

  • Smooth, pore-free surfaces without gaps and dead spaces in the product area.
  • Rounded edges and corners, no screws with open threads in the product area.
  • Parts removable without tools for weekly cleaning: brush sets, film guides, strippers.
  • Draining surfaces so that cleaning agent does not remain standing.
  • Materials that are resistant to the approved cleaning agents.
  • Separation of product area and drive area by seals and fixed guards.

The implementation of the principles was checked during certification on one machine of each type in the Pharma-GMP configuration. The check comprised a visual inspection of all product-contact assemblies, a cleaning trial with a test product and the verification of the material certificates. The results are documented in the certification report, which is part of the technical file.

6 Obligations of the operator

The certificate applies only as long as the operator uses the approved cleaning agents, observes the cleaning intervals, installs original spare parts and agrees changes to product-contact assemblies with Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG in advance. Worn product-contact parts, such as sealing jaws with damaged coating or film guides with scoring, must be replaced because damaged surfaces impair cleanability. The operator informs Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG if a cleaning agent outside the approval list is to be used; the approval is given in writing after a resistance test.

7 Validity and renewal

The certificate is valid until 31.03.2028. Renewal takes place after a renewed check of the design principles on one machine of each type and after updating of the material certificates. Changes to product-contact assemblies that Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG makes to the series lead to a new version of the certificate; new versions are published in the myTannwald portal with a change note. The function current versions only of the portal no longer shows expired certificates; they remain accessible via the change history.

8 Terms

Product-contact is an assembly that can come into contact with the product or with the inside of the base film. The product area is the space of the machine in which product or open cavities are located; the drive area is the separate space with drives, pneumatics and electrical equipment. Approved plastic is a material for which a material certificate on the suitability for contact with pharmaceutical products exists. Removable without tools is a part that can be taken out and reinserted for cleaning without tools; the protective hoods are not among them, they are protective devices and are opened only when the machine is at a standstill.

Questions on this certificate are answered by the support of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG. The assistant in the myTannwald portal answers questions on materials, assemblies and the cleaning concept from this certificate and cites the chapter; for safety-related questions on cleaning it refers to the safety chapter of the respective operating instructions.

10 Cleaning agents and resistance

The approval of the cleaning agents RM-12, RM-20 and RM-05 for the product-contact assemblies is based on resistance tests in which samples of the materials were exposed to the agent for a defined period and then checked for discolouration, swelling, cracking and change of weight. All materials according to chapter 3 passed the test with the approved agents. Abrasive pastes, solvents and chlorine-containing cleaners have not been tested and are therefore not approved; they can attack the coating of the sealing jaw and the forming plate and make the brushes brittle.

The lubricant RM-31 is food-compatible and approved for guides outside the product area; it is applied sparingly so that it does not reach the product area. For the KT-500 cartoner, the cleaner RM-40 for the glue system applies in addition; it is not approved for the blister lines. An operator who wishes to use a further agent, for example a disinfectant prescribed in the clean room, informs Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG; the approval is given in writing after a resistance test and is included in the next version of the maintenance schedule.

Cleaning agents are never used while the machine is switched on and never on hot parts. This rule belongs to the safety chapter of the operating instructions and is repeated here because questions on cleaning in the portal are often safety-related: in these cases the assistant refers to the safety chapter and names the cleaning agents only from the maintenance schedule.
