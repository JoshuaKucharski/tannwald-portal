1 Scope and basics

1.1 Purpose of the maintenance schedule

This maintenance schedule defines the maintenance and cleaning tasks for the TB-200 blister line. It supplements the operating instructions TB-200 and is the binding document for intervals, cleaning agents and spare parts within preventive maintenance. The schedule applies to all machines of type TB-200 regardless of configuration; machine-specific additions are given in the machine documentation of the respective serial number.

The intervals are designed for two-shift operation on five days per week. For three-shift operation or for abrasive films, the intervals for the sealing station and the punching station are halved. The operator documents every maintenance task in the maintenance log according to chapter 9 or in the myTannwald portal.

1.2 Qualification

Daily and weekly cleaning tasks are carried out by the trained operator. Monthly and six-monthly checks are carried out by the operator's maintenance personnel. Work on heating cartridges, temperature sensors, safety switches and on the electrical system is reserved for qualified specialists. The yearly service is carried out by Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG and documented in the portal.

1.3 Safety notes

The safety chapter of the operating instructions TB-200, chapter 2, applies to all work. Before any work that involves reaching into the machine, the line must be de-energised, secured against being switched on again and disconnected from the compressed air. Protective hoods are opened only when the machine is at a standstill; the interlock is never bypassed. Hot parts are touched only after cooling down to the cooling value. Protective gloves and safety glasses must be worn during wet cleaning. This maintenance schedule does not repeat the safety rules in full; it presupposes them.

Other agents, in particular abrasive pastes, solvents and chlorine-containing cleaners, are not permitted and can lead to loss of warranty.

3 Sealing station

3.1 Sealing station daily

The sealing station is dry cleaned daily before the start of production. After the sealing jaw has cooled down, sealing jaw and counter plate are freed from film residues and abrasion with a lint-free cloth. Melted film residues are removed with the plastic scraper. At the same time the interlock of the protective hood is checked: open the hood, the machine must stop, the operator panel must display the message. The result is noted in the shift log.

3.2 Sealing station weekly

Weekly, the sealing station is wet cleaned with the cleaning agent RM-12 and the weekly inspection of the sealing jaws is carried out. Worn or dirty sealing jaws lead to an uneven seam and can damage the lidding film. Check the surface weekly; replace the jaws if scored (part 4711). Wet cleaning is carried out with the line de-energised and the protective hood locked according to the operating instructions TB-200, chapter 7.3.

During the inspection the sealing jaw is examined under good lighting and with the magnifier from the tool kit. Even traces of use are permissible; scoring that can be felt with a fingernail, broken edges of the sealing grid and discolouration caused by overheated film are not. A sealing jaw with such damage is replaced by part 4711. After replacement, the sealing jaw is aligned plane-parallel to the counter plate and the seal seam is checked on three blisters.

  • Wet cleaning of sealing jaw, counter plate and film guide with RM-12
  • Visual inspection of the sealing jaw for scoring, chipping and discolouration
  • Check of the heating cartridge connectors for firm seating and discolouration, without intervention
  • Check of the sealing cylinder for leaks by listening with pressure applied after restarting

3.3 Sealing station every six months

Every six months the qualified specialist checks the heating cartridges and temperature sensors of the sealing jaw. The resistance of the heating cartridges is checked against the setpoint from the wiring diagram, the leads are checked for damage and the temperature sensor is checked for plausibility against a reference thermometer. Heating cartridges with deviating resistance or damaged leads are replaced by part 4712, temperature sensors by part 4715. Safety bulletin SB-2026-08 must be observed.

4 Forming station

The forming station is dry cleaned daily and wet cleaned on product change. Monthly, the seal of the forming air is checked and the forming plate is inspected for scratches. Every six months the qualified specialist checks the heating elements of the preheating and the temperature sensor of the forming station using the same procedure as at the sealing station. The cooling plate is checked monthly for flow; a reduced flow indicates deposits in the cooling circuit and must be reported to the maintenance personnel.

5 Unwinding station and film feed

The unwinding station is checked weekly for free movement of the dancer roller. The roller is cleaned with RM-12 and the bearings are lubricated sparingly with RM-31. Monthly, the brake is checked: the brake value set on the operator panel must match the actual film tension; deviations show in the position of the dancer roller during production. The setpoint for the brake value after a roll change is given in service bulletin SRV-2026-19, table 2. The web edge control is checked monthly for dirt on the sensors; the sensors are cleaned with RM-05.

The feed rollers are checked weekly for abrasion and film residues. Rubber-coated rollers with a smooth-worn surface are replaced (part 4731). Uneven feed shows as film running askew or as a wandering print image of the lidding film.

6 Punching station

The punching station is dry cleaned daily; the waste container is emptied at every shift change. Weekly, the guides of the format tool are lubricated with RM-31. Monthly, the format tool is checked for cutting edge wear: a clean cut without burr and without threads is the criterion. Blunt tools are sent to Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG for regrinding or replaced by the format tool of the respective format. The cutting edges are sharp; the check is carried out without touching them by hand.

7 Electrical system and safety devices

The safety switches of the protective hoods are checked daily by the function test according to chapter 3.1. Every six months the qualified specialist additionally checks the emergency stop, the two-hand control in jog mode and the connectors of the heating cartridges. Yearly, as part of the service, the electrical installation is checked according to the wiring diagram of the machine. The results are recorded in the maintenance log. A safety device that fails the check leads to a standstill of the machine until it has been repaired.

9 Maintenance log

Every maintenance task is documented with date, serial number, task carried out, cleaning agent used, parts replaced and signature of the person carrying it out. The log can be kept in the myTannwald portal; there it is assigned to the serial number and can be viewed by the service of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG. Deviations from the schedule, such as shortened intervals for abrasive films, are noted with a reason. The log is part of the machine documentation and must be presented at audits.

The following checklist summarises the tasks per interval and can serve as a template for the shift log.

  1. Daily: dry cleaning of all stations, check of the interlock, visual inspection for film residues and damage.
  2. Weekly: wet cleaning of the sealing station with RM-12, inspection of the sealing jaws, check dancer roller and feed rollers, lubricate guides of the punching station.
  3. Monthly: check brake and web edge control, check forming air seal and flow of the cooling plate, check format tool for cutting edge wear.
  4. Every six months: check heating cartridges and temperature sensors, check emergency stop and two-hand control.
  5. Yearly: service by Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG with check of the electrical installation.

The terms sealing plate and sealing jaw are distinguished: the sealing plate is the format-dependent part with the sealing grid, the sealing jaw is the heated upper part that carries the sealing plate. In the maintenance schedule, inspection of the sealing jaws means the check of both parts, as scoring and chipping can occur on both. On replacement, the sealing jaw with sealing plate is usually replaced as a unit.

The interval terms daily, weekly, monthly, every six months and yearly refer to calendar time in two-shift operation. For a different shift model, the operator converts the intervals to operating hours: daily corresponds to 16 operating hours, weekly to 80 operating hours, monthly to 350 operating hours. The line controller counts the operating hours and shows the due maintenance tasks on the operator panel; the display does not replace the maintenance schedule but serves as a reminder.

11 Using the maintenance schedule in the portal

In the myTannwald portal this maintenance schedule is assigned to the serial number of your machine and is available in German, English and French. The assistant answers questions on intervals, approved cleaning agents and wear parts from this schedule and cites the section. For safety-related questions, for example on opening the protective hood or on de-energising, the assistant refers to the safety chapter of the operating instructions TB-200 and does not give shortened instructions. Due dates from the maintenance log are shown by the portal on the machine page.
