1 Design and function

The TB-200 blister line is a thermoforming packaging machine for solid dosage forms and small medical devices. It forms cavities from a base film, fills them via a feeder, closes them with a lidding film at the sealing station and separates the finished blisters at the punching station. These operating instructions apply to all machines of type TB-200 from year of manufacture 2019 onwards and describe the intended operation by trained personnel.

The instructions are part of the machine. They must be kept at the installation site within easy reach and must be accessible to every person who works on the machine. If the machine is passed on, the instructions must be handed over with it. Changes compared with the previous version are described in the change note in the myTannwald portal.

1.1 Overview of the stations

The TB-200 consists of five stations arranged in the running direction of the film. The unwinding station holds the roll of base film and guides it over a dancer roller with brake into the forming station. In the forming station the film is heated and formed into the forming plate with compressed air. The filling station feeds the product into the cavities by brushes or by a feeder. The sealing station joins base film and lidding film under pressure and heat. The punching station separates the blisters from the film web and transfers them to the downstream cartoner, for example a KT-500.

  • Unwinding station with dancer roller, brake and web edge control
  • Forming station with preheating, forming plate and cooling plate
  • Filling station with brush feeder and fill control
  • Sealing station with heated sealing jaw, counter plate and protective hood
  • Punching station with format tool and outfeed belt

All stations are controlled from the operator panel at the front. The operator panel shows the operating state, faults with error code and the current setpoints of the loaded format. The protective hoods of the forming, sealing and punching stations are fitted with an interlock that stops the movement of the machine when the hood is opened.

2 Safety

This chapter contains the safety rules for all work on the TB-200. Read it completely before you switch on the machine for the first time. The assistant in the myTannwald portal always refers to this chapter for safety-related questions and does not give shortened instructions.

2.1 Basic rules

The machine may only be operated by persons who have been trained by Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG or by the operator and who have read these instructions. Work on the electrical system, the pneumatics and the heating cartridges is reserved for qualified specialists. Unauthorised persons, in particular children and unaccompanied visitors, must not stay in the working area of the machine.

  • Before any work on a station, de-energise the line and secure it against being switched on again.
  • Open protective hoods only when the machine is at a standstill. Never bypass, block or remove the interlock.
  • Do not reach into running stations, not even to remove film residues.
  • Wear personal protective equipment as specified by the operator; wear protective gloves and safety glasses during wet cleaning.
  • Use only approved cleaning agents and original spare parts.

2.2 Protective hood and interlock

The protective hoods of the forming, sealing and punching stations are interlocked by safety switches. If a hood is opened, the machine stops immediately and the operator panel reports the fault code of the station concerned. The interlock is a protective device within the meaning of the declaration of conformity. Operation with a bypassed, blocked or removed interlock is prohibited and leads to loss of conformity. Check the interlock every day before the start of production: open the hood, the machine must stop, the message must appear.

Damaged hoods, loose hinges or malfunctioning safety switches must be reported to the operator immediately. The machine must not be operated until it has been repaired. Information on the protective hood and the sealing station is also given in safety bulletin SB-2026-07.

2.3 De-energising the line

Before cleaning, maintenance, format change and troubleshooting that involves reaching into the machine, the line must be de-energised at the main switch. The main switch must be secured with a padlock against being switched on again; the key stays with the person working on the machine. In addition, the compressed air supply must be disconnected and the residual pressure released. Work may only begin when the operator panel is dark and the pressure gauge shows zero. The exact sequence is defined by the operator in its lockout procedure; these instructions do not replace it.

2.4 Heating cartridges and hot surfaces

Sealing jaw, counter plate and the preheating of the forming station become hot during operation and remain hot for a long time after switching off. Touch these parts only when the temperature display on the operator panel shows the cooling value. Heating cartridges may only be replaced by a qualified specialist; safety bulletin SB-2026-08 must be observed. Damaged leads, discoloured connectors or a smell of overheated plastic are reasons for an immediate shutdown.

2.5 Cleaning agents

Only the cleaning agents approved in the maintenance schedule TB-200, section 2, are permitted for cleaning. Other agents can attack seals, forming plates and the coating of the sealing jaw. Never spray cleaning agents onto hot parts and never use them while the machine is switched on. The safety data sheets of the agents are kept by the operator.

2.6 Crushing and shearing points

Crushing and shearing points exist at the forming station between forming plate and upper part, at the sealing station between sealing jaw and counter plate, at the punching station at the format tool and at all rollers of the film feed. These points are covered by the protective hoods during operation. When setting up in jog mode, keep hands and tools outside the range of movement and do not bypass the two-hand control.

3 Transport and installation

The machine is delivered on a transport pallet with lashing straps. Transport the machine only with an industrial truck of sufficient load capacity and only at the marked lifting points. The installation site must be level, load-bearing and free of vibrations. The clearances for operation, roll change and maintenance given in the installation plan must be observed. After installation, align the machine with the levelling feet and check the spirit level at the forming station.

The electrical connection and the compressed air connection are made by the operator's qualified specialist according to the wiring diagram and the pneumatic diagram of the machine. The wiring diagrams for the high-frequency sealing are subject to export control and are visible in the portal only for approved countries.

4 Commissioning

Initial commissioning is carried out by a service technician of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG together with the operator. The format data are loaded, the safety functions are checked and the acceptance is recorded. The following sequence applies to daily start-up:

  1. Visual inspection of all stations for film residues, foreign objects and damage.
  2. Check of the interlock on all protective hoods according to chapter 2.2.
  3. Switch on the main switch, open the compressed air, start the operator panel.
  4. Select the format and check the setpoints for forming temperature, sealing temperature and film tension.
  5. Wait for the preheating time until all temperature displays are green.
  6. Run an empty cycle without product and check the seal seam on three blisters.

Product is fed only after the seal seam has been checked successfully. The test values must be documented in the shift log.

5 Operation

5.1 Operator panel

The operator panel shows the operating state in the header, the stations with actual values and setpoints in the middle and the current messages in the footer. Faults are displayed with an error code following the pattern E-number; the meaning of the codes is given in chapter 9. Production is started with Start and stopped in a controlled way with Stop. The emergency stop stops all movements immediately and switches off the heaters; it may only be used in case of danger.

5.2 Roll change

The roll change at the unwinding station is carried out with the machine at a standstill. The remaining roll is taken out of the holder, the new roll is inserted and the film is threaded over the dancer roller. After threading, the brake of the unwinding station must be set to the setpoint of the new roll. A brake value that still belongs to the previous roll leads to incorrect film tension and to faults at the sealing station. The current setpoint is given in service bulletin SRV-2026-19, table 2.

5.3 Production mode

In production mode the control system monitors temperatures, film tension, fill control and sealing pressure. If a value deviates from the setpoint, a warning appears first and, after the tolerance time has elapsed, a fault with stop. The operator checks the seal seam and the punch cut on three blisters every hour and records the result in the shift log.

6 Format change

A format change comprises the replacement of forming plate, sealing plate and punching tool as well as loading of the format data set. The format change is carried out with the line de-energised according to chapter 2.3. The format parts are marked with the format number; only parts with the same number may be combined. After installation the machine is run three cycles in jog mode before the hoods are closed and the empty cycle is started. Details on formats and tools are given in the training document TB-200 format change.

7.2 Cleaning the forming station

The forming station is dry cleaned daily. Film residues and abrasion are removed from the forming plate and the cooling plate with a lint-free cloth and a soft brush. The forming plate must not be cleaned with hard objects because scratches impair the cavity quality. Wet cleaning of the forming station is only required on product change and is carried out with the approved cleaning agent RM-12.

7.3 Cleaning the sealing station

The sealing station is dry cleaned daily and wet cleaned weekly. During daily dry cleaning, sealing jaw and counter plate are freed from film residues and abrasion with a lint-free cloth after cooling down. Melted film residues are removed with the plastic scraper from the tool kit, never with metal tools.

Before weekly wet cleaning, the line must be de-energised and the protective hood of the sealing station must be locked so that it cannot be closed accidentally and the machine cannot be started during the work. Only then are sealing jaw, counter plate and film guide cleaned with the cleaning agent RM-12. The sealing jaw must have cooled down to the cooling value before wet cleaning. After cleaning, all parts are wiped dry and the surface of the sealing jaw is checked for scoring.

Use only the cleaning agents approved in the maintenance schedule TB-200, section 2. Do not let cleaning agent get into the heating cartridge bores or onto the connectors. After drying, the protective hood is unlocked, the line is switched on and the sealing station is preheated to operating temperature. The first three blisters after a wet cleaning must be rejected and the seal seam must be checked.

7.4 Cleaning the punching station

The punching station is dry cleaned daily. Punching waste is removed from the waste container and the format tool is freed from film dust with the brush. The cutting edges of the format tool are sharp; cleaning is done only with brush and cloth, not by hand.

8 Spare parts

Spare parts are listed in the spare parts catalogue TB-200 with part number, designation and installation location. Use only original spare parts from Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG. For orders and questions about availability, contact support; the instructions contain no information on delivery times or prices. Wear parts such as sealing jaws, heating cartridges and temperature sensors should be kept in stock at the site.

9.2 Faults at the forming station

Incompletely formed cavities occur when the forming temperature is too low, the forming air is switched off too early or the forming plate is dirty. First check the temperature display and the preheating time. If the cavity quality remains poor, the forming plate must be cleaned and the seal of the forming air must be checked. White spots at the cavity edges indicate a film that is too cold; translucent cavities with a thin bottom indicate a film that is too hot.

9.3 Faults at the film feed

If the film runs askew or folds form in front of the forming station, the web edge control must be checked and the dancer roller must be checked for free movement. A dirty or damaged roll holder leads to uneven unwinding. After each roll change, the film position at the forming station must be checked before production is started.

9.4 Faults at the sealing station: leaking seal seam, film tear

If the lidding film tears in the sealing area, the film tension after the roll change is usually too high or the sealing temperature is above the setpoint of the film specification. First check the brake of the unwinding station: if the brake value is still set to the value of the previous roll, the lidding film is overstretched in the sealing area and tears at the edge of the sealing jaw. Set the brake value to the setpoint given in service bulletin SRV-2026-19 and run three empty cycles.

If the film tension is correct, check the sealing temperature on the operator panel. If the actual value is above the setpoint of the film specification, the lidding film becomes too soft and is pulled along when the sealing jaw opens. Let the sealing jaw cool down to the setpoint and check the temperature sensor. A sensor that reports values that are too low causes the control system to keep heating although the jaw is already too hot.

A leaking seal seam usually has one of the following causes: dirty or worn sealing jaw, sealing temperature too low, sealing pressure too low or a lidding film that does not match the base film. Check the sealing jaw for scoring and film residues and clean it according to chapter 7.3. Worn sealing jaws are replaced by part 4711 from the spare parts catalogue. A leak test of the blisters must be carried out after every change at the sealing station.

10 Decommissioning and disposal

For a longer standstill the machine is de-energised, disconnected from the compressed air, cleaned and covered against dust. Film rolls are removed and stored according to the manufacturer's instructions. On final decommissioning, electronics, metals and plastics must be separated and sent for recycling; the heating cartridges and temperature sensors belong to electronic waste. The operator documents the decommissioning in the myTannwald portal so that the document assignment to the serial number can be updated.
