1 Scope and export control

This wiring diagram describes the electrical structure of the KT-500 cartoner. It covers the control cabinet, the operator panel, the safety chain with the protective hoods, the drives, the sensors of the carton feed and the leaflet folder, the glue system option and the coupling interface to an upstream blister line. The document is intended for qualified electrical specialists of the operator and for the service of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG. It contains no commissioning procedure and no instructions for modifying the safety chain.

The wiring diagram KT-500 is classified as export class 1. In the myTannwald portal it is visible for users in countries that are approved for export class 1 according to the export rules of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG; every access is recorded in the audit log. Copying or forwarding this document outside the portal requires written approval of the compliance department.

2 Safety

Work on the electrical system of the KT-500 is reserved for qualified electrical specialists. The safety chapter of the operating instructions KT-500 applies in full. Before any work in the control cabinet, the line must be de-energised at the main switch, secured against being switched on again and the absence of voltage must be verified by the specialist. In a coupled line, the blister line is de-energised as well because its outfeed belt extends into the product infeed. The control cabinet remains closed during operation; its door is interlocked and opening it stops the machine.

The safety chain of the KT-500 comprises the three protective hoods, the emergency stop buttons and the cabinet door. Bypassing, bridging or modifying any element of the safety chain is prohibited; it leads to loss of conformity and exposes persons to the crushing points of the insertion fingers, the suction cups, the folding rollers and the folding tools. This document does not describe how safety functions can be overridden for test purposes; test modes are available to Tannwald service only. The heater of the glue system remains live until the main switch is switched off; the operator panel alone does not de-energise it.

3 Control cabinet and assemblies

3.1 Control cabinet

The control cabinet is mounted at the rear of the closing station and contains the line controller KT-500, the safety relay, the drive controllers, the temperature controller of the glue system option, the coupling module to the blister line and the terminal strips. The cabinet has a mains disconnect switch that serves as the main switch of the machine and can be secured with a padlock. A cooling fan with filter keeps the cabinet temperature within range; the filter is replaced every six months.

3.2 Operator panel

The operator panel is mounted at the front of the insertion station and connected to the line controller by a shielded cable. It carries the emergency stop button, which is wired directly into the safety chain. The operator panel displays the operating state, the fill levels of carton magazine and leaflet folder and the current messages; it has no safety function of its own apart from the emergency stop button.

3.3 Drives

The infeed chain, the carton feed rotor, the leaflet folder, the insertion station and the closing station are driven by servo drives with drive controllers in the control cabinet. The drive controllers receive the enable from the safety chain; without enable, no drive can move. The drive controllers report their state to the line controller via the fieldbus; the enable itself is hard-wired.

3.4 Vacuum unit

The vacuum pump of the carton feed is controlled by the line controller and monitored by a vacuum switch. A vacuum below the threshold stops the carton feed and reports a fault; the suction cups are then checked according to the maintenance schedule KT-500. The vacuum switch is not part of the safety chain.

6 Glue system option

Machines with the glue system option have a heated glue tank with nozzles at the closing station. The heater is controlled by a temperature controller in the control cabinet and switched by a solid-state relay. The heater receives the enable S_ENABLE_GLUE from the safety chain; it is switched off when the safety chain opens and is not released again until the fault has been reset. The temperature sensor of the glue tank is monitored by the controller; a sensor fault switches the heater off and reports a fault.

7 Coupling interface to the blister line

The KT-500 is coupled to an upstream TB-200 or TB-400 blister line via the coupling module in the control cabinet and the terminal strip X6. The interface transmits start, stop, readiness and the safety state of both machines. The signal S_LINE_OK of the KT-500 and the corresponding signal of the blister line are exchanged so that opening a hood on one machine stops both machines. The KT-500 reports belt full to the blister line when the product infeed cannot accept further blisters.
