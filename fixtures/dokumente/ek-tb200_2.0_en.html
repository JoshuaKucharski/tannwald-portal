1 Notes on the catalogue

The spare parts catalogue TB-200 contains all spare and wear parts of the TB-200 blister line, arranged by station. Each part is listed with part number, designation, installation location and notes on replacement. The part numbers apply to all machines of type TB-200 from year of manufacture 2019 onwards; deviating parts for older machines or customer-specific formats are given in the machine documentation of the respective serial number.

Orders are placed via the support of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG, stating the serial number and the part number. The catalogue contains no information on prices, availability or delivery times; this information is provided by support on request. Only original spare parts are approved for the TB-200. The installation of third-party parts leads to loss of conformity and warranty.

1.1 Structure of the part numbers

The part numbers have four digits. The first digit designates the station: 4 for sealing and forming station, 5 for punching station, 3 for unwinding station and film feed, 6 for control system and electrical equipment, 7 for general parts and tools. Format-dependent parts additionally carry the format number engraved on the format part. For format-dependent parts, the format number must be stated when ordering.

1.2 Safety notes on part replacement

The safety chapter of the operating instructions TB-200, chapter 2, applies to every part replacement. The line must be de-energised and secured against being switched on again, and the compressed air must be disconnected. Heating cartridges, temperature sensors, safety switches and electrical parts may only be replaced by a qualified specialist. Hot parts are touched only after cooling down. This catalogue does not describe work steps; it names the parts and refers to the operating instructions and the maintenance schedule.

4 Sealing station

The sealing station joins base film and lidding film under pressure and heat. Its parts are subject to the highest thermal and mechanical loads; sealing jaws, heating cartridges and temperature sensors are wear parts and should be kept in stock at the site.

4.1 Sealing jaws and heating cartridges

The sealing jaw carries the sealing grid and is heated by two heating cartridges; the temperature sensor sits in a separate bore in the centre of the jaw. The sealing jaw is coated and may only be cleaned with the plastic scraper and the approved cleaning agents. Worn sealing jaws with scoring, broken edges of the sealing grid or discolouration must be replaced according to the maintenance schedule TB-200, section 3.2.

8 Ordering and return

Orders contain serial number, part number, designation, quantity and, for format-dependent parts, the format number. Support confirms the order and states availability and delivery date; the catalogue itself gives no information on this. Defective parts replaced under warranty must be returned to Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG with the return note. Heating cartridges and temperature sensors are marked with the part number and the serial number of the machine before return.

For machines with a Service Plus contract, wear parts of the sealing station are included in the scope of the contract; the scope is noted in the machine documentation of the serial number. For machines with a basic contract, wear parts are invoiced individually.

9 Wear parts and stocking

Wear parts are parts whose service life is limited by operation as planned. For the TB-200 these include the sealing jaw, the heating cartridges, the temperature sensors, the brake linings of the unwinding station, the rubber-coated feed rollers, the strippers and the conveyor belt of the punching station as well as the seals of forming air and sealing cylinder. These parts are marked as wear parts in the tables of chapters 2 to 5.

Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG recommends keeping a basic stock of wear parts per machine at the site so that faults at the sealing station can be remedied without loss of production. The recommended basic stock depends on shift model and film material and is given in the following table for two-shift operation. For three-shift operation and abrasive films, the quantities for the sealing station are doubled.

Wear parts are stored dry, dust-free and in their original packaging. Sealing jaws are stored lying flat so that the coating is not damaged; heating cartridges and temperature sensors remain in their protective sleeve until installation. Parts with damaged packaging must be checked for transport damage before installation. The storage time is not limited for the parts mentioned; seals should be installed within five years.

The consumption of wear parts is recorded in the maintenance log. A conspicuously high consumption of sealing jaws indicates a sealing temperature that is too high, an incorrect film specification or improper cleaning and should be reported to the service of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG. A high consumption of brake linings indicates brake values that are too high after the roll change; the setpoint is described in service bulletin SRV-2026-19.

10 Format-dependent parts

Format-dependent parts are tailored to a specific blister format and carry the format number. Each format comprises the forming plate of the forming station, the sealing plate with the sealing grid, the format tool of the punching station and, if required, a format-specific film guide. These parts are managed together as a format set and may only be combined within the same format set. A format set is delivered complete when a new format is ordered and is assigned to the serial number in the myTannwald portal.

Format-dependent parts are stored on the format trolley and brought to the machine together at format change. Damaged format parts, in particular forming plates with scratches and punching tools with broken cutting edges, are not reused but sent to Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG for repair. The repair of format tools comprises regrinding of the cutting edges and checking of the guides; the format number is retained.

For customer-specific formats not included in the standard range, the part numbers from the machine documentation of the respective serial number apply. This documentation is visible in the portal only for the owning customer company. Direct questions on customer-specific formats to support, stating the serial number.

11 Using the catalogue in the portal

In the myTannwald portal this catalogue is assigned to the serial number of your machine and is available in German and English. The search in the portal finds part numbers directly: entering a four-digit part number leads to the table row with designation and installation location. The assistant answers questions on designation, installation location and replacement notes from this catalogue and cites the chapter. Questions on prices, availability and delivery times cannot be answered by the assistant from the catalogue; in this case it offers a support request that transmits question, history and serial number to support.

New versions of the catalogue are published in the portal with a change note. The change history shows which part numbers have been added, replaced or deleted. Replaced part numbers remain in the change history with a reference to the successor number so that older orders remain traceable.
