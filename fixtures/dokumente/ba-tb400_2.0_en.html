1 Design and function

The TB-400 blister line is a thermoforming packaging machine with two parallel film webs and a working width of 400 mm. It forms cavities from two base films, fills them via two feeders, closes them at the sealing station with one lidding film each and separates the blisters at the punching station. These operating instructions apply to all machines of type TB-400 from year of manufacture 2020 onwards and describe the intended operation by trained personnel.

The instructions are part of the machine and must be kept within easy reach at the installation site. Version 2.0 completely replaces version 1.x; changes are given in the change note in the myTannwald portal. The training document TB-400 operator training is available in addition for operators.

1.1 Overview of the stations

The TB-400 consists of five stations, each designed for two film webs. The unwinding station holds two rolls and guides both webs over one dancer roller with brake each into the forming station. The forming station heats both webs with a common preheating and forms them into two forming plates. The filling station feeds the product via two brush feeders; a common fill control monitors both webs. The sealing station has two sealing jaws and two counter plates. The punching station separates the blisters of both webs with two format tools and places them on a common outfeed belt.

  • Unwinding station with two roll holders, two brakes and web edge control per web
  • Forming station with common preheating, two forming plates and one cooling plate
  • Filling station with two brush feeders and common fill control
  • Sealing station with two heated sealing jaws, two counter plates and two protective hoods
  • Punching station with two format tools and common outfeed belt

2 Safety

This chapter contains the safety rules for all work on the TB-400. Read it completely before you switch on the machine for the first time. The assistant in the myTannwald portal refers to this chapter for safety-related questions and does not give shortened instructions.

2.1 Basic rules

The machine may only be operated by persons who have been trained and who have read these instructions. Work on the electrical system, the pneumatics and the heating cartridges is reserved for qualified specialists. Unauthorised persons, in particular children and unaccompanied visitors, must not stay in the working area.

  • Before any work on a station, de-energise the line, secure it against being switched on again and disconnect the compressed air.
  • Open protective hoods only when the machine is at a standstill. Never bypass, block or remove the interlock.
  • Do not reach into running stations, not even on the second web while the first one is stopped.
  • Wear personal protective equipment as specified by the operator; wear protective gloves and safety glasses during wet cleaning.
  • Use only approved cleaning agents and original spare parts.

2.2 Protective hoods and interlock

Each station of the TB-400 has two protective hoods, one per web. All hoods are interlocked by safety switches. Opening a hood stops the whole machine, that is both webs; the operator panel reports the fault code with the number of the hood. Operation with a bypassed, blocked or removed interlock is prohibited and leads to loss of conformity. The interlock is checked every day before the start of production on all hoods: open the hood, the machine must stop, the message must appear.

2.3 De-energising the line

Before cleaning, maintenance, format change and troubleshooting that involves reaching into the machine, the line must be de-energised at the main switch and secured with a padlock against being switched on again. The compressed air must be disconnected and the residual pressure released. Work may only begin when the operator panel is dark and the pressure gauge shows zero. The operator's lockout procedure defines the exact sequence; these instructions do not replace it.

2.4 Heating cartridges and hot surfaces

Both sealing jaws, both counter plates and the preheating become hot during operation and remain hot for a long time after switching off. Touch these parts only when the temperature display on the operator panel shows the cooling value for both webs. Heating cartridges may only be replaced by a qualified specialist. Damaged leads, discoloured connectors or a smell of overheated plastic are reasons for an immediate shutdown.

2.5 Cleaning agents and crushing points

Only the cleaning agents approved in the maintenance schedule TB-400, section 2, are permitted for cleaning. Never spray cleaning agents onto hot parts and never use them while the machine is switched on. Crushing and shearing points exist on both webs between forming plate and upper part, between sealing jaw and counter plate, at the format tool and at all rollers. When setting up in jog mode, keep hands and tools outside the range of movement of both webs and do not bypass the two-hand control.

3 Transport and installation

The TB-400 is delivered on two transport pallets: unwinding and forming station on the first, filling, sealing and punching station on the second. Transport the assemblies only with an industrial truck of sufficient load capacity at the marked lifting points. The installation site must be level, load-bearing and free of vibrations; the clearances in the installation plan take into account the roll change on both webs from the operator side. After joining the assemblies, the machine is aligned with the levelling feet and the web position of both webs is checked at the forming station. The electrical connection and the compressed air connection are made by the operator's qualified specialist according to the wiring diagram TB-400 and the pneumatic diagram.

4 Commissioning

Initial commissioning is carried out by a service technician of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG together with the operator. The following sequence applies to daily start-up:

  1. Visual inspection of all stations on both webs for film residues, foreign objects and damage.
  2. Check of the interlock on all protective hoods according to chapter 2.2.
  3. Switch on the main switch, open the compressed air, start the operator panel.
  4. Select the format and check the setpoints for both webs: forming temperature, sealing temperature per web, film tension per web.
  5. Wait for the preheating time until all temperature displays of both webs are green.
  6. Run an empty cycle without product and check the seal seam on three blisters per web.

5 Operation

5.1 Operator panel with two webs

The operator panel shows both webs side by side with actual values and setpoints per station. Faults are displayed with an error code following the pattern T-number and with the web number; the meaning of the codes is given in chapter 9. Start and Stop always act on both webs. One web can be deselected for idling, for example during a roll change on the other web; the deselected web then runs without film and without product.

5.2 Roll change on both webs

The roll change is carried out with the machine at a standstill, separately for each web. After threading, the brake of the respective web must be set to the setpoint of the new roll. A brake value that still belongs to the previous roll leads to incorrect film tension and to faults at the sealing station on that web. The setpoint is given in the format data set; for the TB-200 it is described in service bulletin SRV-2026-19, whose notes also apply analogously to the TB-400.

5.3 Production mode

In production mode the control system monitors temperatures, film tension, fill control and sealing pressure per web. If a value deviates from the setpoint, a warning with web number appears first and, after the tolerance time has elapsed, a fault with stop of both webs. The operator checks the seal seam and the punch cut on three blisters per web every hour.

6 Format change

A format change on the TB-400 comprises the replacement of two forming plates, two sealing plates and two format tools as well as loading of the format data set. Both webs always run the same format; the format parts of both webs carry the same format number and are additionally marked with the web number. The format change is carried out with the line de-energised according to chapter 2.3. After installation the machine is run three cycles in jog mode before the hoods are closed and the empty cycle is started.

7 Cleaning and maintenance

7.1 Maintenance intervals

The maintenance schedule TB-400 is the binding document for intervals, cleaning agents and parts. The intervals correspond to those of the TB-200: daily dry cleaning and check of the interlock, weekly wet cleaning of the sealing station and inspection of the sealing jaws of both webs, monthly check of the brakes and the web edge control, six-monthly check of heating cartridges and temperature sensors, yearly service by Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG.

7.2 Cleaning the sealing station

The sealing station is dry cleaned daily and wet cleaned weekly, on both webs. During dry cleaning, both sealing jaws and counter plates are freed from film residues with a lint-free cloth after cooling down; melted residues are removed with the plastic scraper. Before wet cleaning, the line must be de-energised and both protective hoods of the sealing station must be locked. Wet cleaning is carried out with the cleaning agent RM-12; after drying, the sealing jaws of both webs are checked for scoring. The first three blisters per web after a wet cleaning must be rejected.

7.3 Cleaning the other stations

Forming station, filling station and punching station are dry cleaned daily. The forming plates of both webs must not be cleaned with hard objects. The brushes of the filling station are removed weekly, tapped out and checked for wear. The punching waste of both webs falls into a common waste container that is emptied at every shift change.

8 Spare parts

Spare parts are listed in the spare parts catalogue TB-400 with part number, designation and installation location; parts that exist per web carry the note two pieces per machine. Use only original spare parts. For orders and questions about availability, contact support; the instructions contain no information on delivery times or prices.

9.2 Faults on one web only

If a fault occurs on one web only, the cause is almost always a part that exists per web: brake, forming plate, brush feeder, sealing jaw or format tool of that web. Compare the actual values and setpoints of both webs on the operator panel; a deviation on one web only identifies the station. After a roll change, the most frequent cause is a brake value that has not been adjusted. After a format change, the most frequent cause is a format part swapped between the webs; check the web number on the format parts.

9.3 Faults at the sealing station

A leaking seal seam on one web usually has a dirty or worn sealing jaw of that web, a sealing temperature of that web that is too low or a sealing pressure that is too low as its cause. If the lidding film of one web tears in the sealing area, the film tension of that web after the roll change is usually too high or the sealing temperature of that web is above the setpoint. First check the brake value and then the sealing temperature of the affected web; let the sealing jaw cool down to the setpoint and clean it according to chapter 7.2. Worn sealing jaws of the TB-400 are replaced by the part from the spare parts catalogue TB-400, chapter 4.

10 Decommissioning and disposal

For a longer standstill the machine is de-energised, disconnected from the compressed air, cleaned on both webs and covered against dust. On final decommissioning, electronics, metals and plastics must be separated and sent for recycling. The operator documents the decommissioning in the myTannwald portal so that the document assignment to the serial number can be updated.
