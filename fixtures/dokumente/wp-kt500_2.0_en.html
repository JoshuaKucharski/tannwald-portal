1 Scope and basics

1.1 Purpose of the maintenance schedule

This maintenance schedule defines the maintenance and cleaning tasks for the KT-500 cartoner. It supplements the operating instructions KT-500 and is the binding document for intervals, cleaning agents and spare parts within preventive maintenance. The schedule applies to all machines of type KT-500 regardless of configuration; machine-specific additions are given in the machine documentation of the respective serial number. Version 2.0 introduces the calibration of the carton detection cell and the revised intervals for the leaflet feeder.

The intervals are designed for two-shift operation on five days per week. For three-shift operation or for carton qualities with high dust generation, the intervals for the carton magazine and the closing station are halved. The operator documents every maintenance task in the maintenance log according to chapter 9 or in the myTannwald portal.

1.2 Qualification

Daily and weekly cleaning tasks are carried out by the trained operator. Monthly and six-monthly checks are carried out by the operator's maintenance personnel. Work on drives, sensors, safety switches and on the electrical system is reserved for qualified specialists. The yearly service is carried out by Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG and documented in the portal.

1.3 Safety notes

The safety chapter of the operating instructions KT-500, chapter 2, applies to all work. Before any work that involves reaching into the machine, the line must be de-energised, secured against being switched on again and disconnected from the compressed air. Protective hoods are opened only when the machine is at a standstill; the interlock is never bypassed. The folding and closing stations contain crushing points that are covered by the hoods during operation. Protective gloves must be worn when handling the carton magazine and the leaflet feeder because carton edges are sharp. This maintenance schedule does not repeat the safety rules in full; it presupposes them.

Only the cleaning agents listed in the following table are approved for cleaning the KT-500. They have been tested for compatibility with the plastic guides, the suction cups of the carton feed and the glue system. Other agents, in particular solvents and abrasive pastes, are not permitted and can lead to loss of warranty.

3 Product infeed and carton detection

3.1 Product infeed daily

The product infeed is dry cleaned daily. Blister residues and dust are removed from the infeed chain and the pushers with a brush. The sensors of the product infeed are checked for dirt and wiped with RM-05 if necessary. The interlock of the infeed hood is checked: open the hood, the machine must stop, the operator panel must display the message.

3.2 Calibration of the carton detection cell

The carton detection cell detects whether a carton has been erected correctly before the product is pushed in. It is calibrated weekly and after every carton quality change. Calibration is started from the operator panel in the maintenance menu; the machine must be at a standstill and the hood of the carton feed must be closed. The calibration run uses a reference carton of the loaded format that is placed in the calibration position. The operator panel reports calibration successful or calibration failed. A failed calibration is repeated after cleaning the cell with RM-05; if it fails again, the cell must be checked by a specialist.

  • Clean the carton detection cell with a cloth moistened with RM-05
  • Place the reference carton of the loaded format in the calibration position
  • Start the calibration from the maintenance menu of the operator panel
  • Note the result in the shift log; repeat after cleaning if the calibration failed

The calibration values are stored in the format data set. A format change loads the calibration values of the new format; a calibration after the format change is only required if the carton quality has changed. The procedure replaces the manual adjustment described in version 1.0 of this schedule.

4 Carton magazine and carton feed

The carton magazine is dry cleaned daily; carton dust is removed from the magazine guides and the suction cups are checked for damage. Weekly, the suction cups are cleaned with RM-12 and the vacuum hoses are checked for leaks by listening with vacuum applied. Monthly, the magazine guides are checked for wear and lubricated with RM-31; worn suction cups are replaced (part 2210). The carton feed rotor is checked monthly for free movement.

5 Leaflet feeder

The leaflet feeder folds and inserts the leaflet into the carton. It is dry cleaned daily; paper dust is removed from the folding rollers with a brush. Weekly, the folding rollers are checked for paper residues and the leaflet detection sensor is wiped with RM-05. Monthly, the folding rollers are checked for wear: a leaflet that is folded askew or jams indicates worn rollers, which are replaced in pairs (part 2320). Version 2.0 shortens the interval for the roller check from quarterly to monthly because of the experience with thin leaflet papers.

6 Folding and closing station

The folding and closing station is dry cleaned daily. Weekly, the guides are lubricated with RM-31 and the closing tongues are checked for wear. For machines with glue closing, the glue nozzles are cleaned weekly with RM-40 when the glue system is cold; the glue tank is cleaned monthly. Monthly, the folding station is checked for correct carton closure: a carton that opens again after closing indicates worn closing tongues (part 2410) or incorrectly set glue application. Every six months the specialist checks the drive of the closing station and the safety switches of the hoods.

7 Electrical system and safety devices

The safety switches of the protective hoods are checked daily by the function test according to chapter 3.1. Every six months the qualified specialist additionally checks the emergency stop and the connectors of the sensors. Yearly, as part of the service, the electrical installation is checked according to the wiring diagram of the machine. The results are recorded in the maintenance log. A safety device that fails the check leads to a standstill of the machine until it has been repaired.

9 Maintenance log

Every maintenance task is documented with date, serial number, task carried out, cleaning agent used, parts replaced and signature of the person carrying it out. The calibration of the carton detection cell is documented with the result reported by the operator panel. The log can be kept in the myTannwald portal; there it is assigned to the serial number and can be viewed by the service of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG. The log is part of the machine documentation and must be presented at audits.

  1. Daily: dry cleaning of all stations, check of the interlock, check of suction cups and sensors.
  2. Weekly: calibration of the carton detection cell, clean suction cups, check folding rollers, lubricate guides, clean glue nozzles.
  3. Monthly: check magazine guides and rotor, check folding rollers for wear, check closing tongues, clean glue tank.
  4. Every six months: check vacuum pump, drive of the closing station, safety switches and emergency stop.
  5. Yearly: service by Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG with check of the electrical installation.

10 Changes in version 2.0

Version 2.0 of this maintenance schedule replaces version 1.0 of 2025. The main changes are the calibration procedure of the carton detection cell in chapter 3.2, which replaces the manual adjustment, the shortened interval for the folding roller check of the leaflet feeder in chapter 5 and the addition of the glue system cleaner RM-40 to the approved cleaning agents in chapter 2. The parts list in chapter 8 has been aligned with the spare parts catalogue KT-500. The revision was prepared by the service technician Marco Steiner on the basis of the service reports of 2025 and 2026 and released by the technical editorial office.

Operators who still use version 1.0 should switch to the calibration procedure of version 2.0 at the next weekly maintenance; the manual adjustment remains possible but is no longer described. The myTannwald portal shows the change history of this document and marks version 2.0 as the current version for all machines of type KT-500.
