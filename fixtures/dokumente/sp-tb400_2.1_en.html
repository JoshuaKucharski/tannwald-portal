1 Scope and export control

This wiring diagram describes the electrical structure of the TB-400 blister line with two film webs. It covers the main control cabinet, the operator panel, the temperature control of forming and sealing station for both webs, the safety chain with the ten protective hoods and the interfaces to a downstream cartoner. The document is intended for qualified electrical specialists of the operator and for the service of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG. It contains no commissioning procedure and no instructions for modifying the safety chain.

The wiring diagram TB-400 is classified as export class 1. In the myTannwald portal it is visible for users in countries that are approved for export class 1 according to the export rules of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG; every access is recorded in the audit log. Copying or forwarding this document outside the portal requires written approval of the compliance department.

2 Safety

Work on the electrical system of the TB-400 is reserved for qualified electrical specialists. The safety chapter of the operating instructions TB-400 applies in full. Before any work in the main control cabinet, the line must be de-energised at the main switch, secured against being switched on again and the absence of voltage must be verified by the specialist. The main control cabinet remains closed during operation; its door is interlocked and opening it stops the machine.

The safety chain of the TB-400 comprises the ten protective hoods, the emergency stop buttons, the two-hand control for jog mode and the cabinet door. Bypassing, bridging or modifying any element of the safety chain is prohibited; it leads to loss of conformity and exposes persons to the crushing points of both webs and to the hot sealing jaws. This document does not describe how safety functions can be overridden for test purposes; test modes are available to Tannwald service only. The heating circuits of the sealing jaws remain live until the main switch is switched off; the operator panel alone does not de-energise them.

3 Cabinets and assemblies

3.1 Main control cabinet

The main control cabinet is mounted at the rear of the forming station and contains the line controller TB-400, the safety relay with ten hood inputs, the temperature controllers for sealing station (two channels) and forming station, the drive controllers of the film feed and the terminal strips listed in chapter 6. The cabinet has a mains disconnect switch that serves as the main switch of the line and can be secured with a padlock. A cooling fan with filter keeps the cabinet temperature within range; the filter is replaced every six months.

3.2 Operator panel

The operator panel is mounted at the front of the filling station and connected to the line controller by a shielded cable. It carries the emergency stop button and the two-hand control for jog mode. The operator panel displays both webs side by side; it has no safety function of its own apart from the emergency stop button, which is wired directly into the safety chain.

3.3 Heating circuits

The sealing station has two heating circuits, one per web, each with two heating cartridges and one temperature sensor. The forming station has one heating circuit for the common preheating with one temperature sensor. The heating circuits are switched by solid-state relays in the main control cabinet and monitored by the temperature controllers. A sensor fault switches the affected heating circuit off and reports a fault; the heating circuit is not released again until the fault has been reset by the specialist.

3.4 Drives

The film feed of both webs, the forming station, the sealing station and the punching station are driven by servo drives with drive controllers in the main control cabinet. The drive controllers receive the enable from the safety chain; without enable, no drive can move, not even in jog mode. The drive controllers report their state to the line controller via the fieldbus; the enable itself is hard-wired.

5 Temperature control of both webs

The temperature controller of the sealing station has two channels, one per web, each with its own setpoint from the format data set. The setpoints can be adjusted per web on the operator panel within the tolerance band of the format data set. The controller monitors the actual value against the setpoint and reports a fault with web number when the actual value leaves the tolerance band for longer than the tolerance time. The forming station has one channel for the common preheating.

The resistance of the heating cartridges and the characteristic of the temperature sensors are checked every six months by the specialist according to the maintenance schedule TB-400. The reference values are given on the diagram sheets T-10 to T-20 of the machine-specific documentation. A heating cartridge with deviating resistance is replaced; the heating circuit is not operated with one cartridge only because the sealing jaw would then be heated unevenly.

7 Interface to the cartoner

The TB-400 is coupled to a downstream KT-500 cartoner via the terminal strip X6. The interface transmits start, stop, readiness and the safety state of both machines. The signal S_LINE_OK of the TB-400 and the corresponding signal of the KT-500 are exchanged so that opening a hood on one machine stops both machines. The transfer of blisters is monitored by the product detection of the KT-500; the TB-400 reports the fault transfer to cartoner disturbed when the outfeed belt is full.
