1 Scope and basics

1.1 Purpose of the maintenance schedule

This maintenance schedule defines the maintenance and cleaning tasks for the KT-500 cartoner. It supplements the operating instructions KT-500 and is the binding document for intervals, cleaning agents and spare parts within preventive maintenance. The schedule applies to all machines of type KT-500 regardless of configuration; machine-specific additions are given in the machine documentation of the respective serial number. Version 1.0 is the first edition of this schedule for the KT-500 series.

The intervals are designed for two-shift operation on five days per week. For three-shift operation or for carton qualities with high dust generation, the intervals for the carton magazine and the closing station are halved. The operator documents every maintenance task in the maintenance log according to chapter 9 or in the myTannwald portal.

1.2 Qualification

Daily and weekly cleaning tasks are carried out by the trained operator. Monthly and six-monthly checks are carried out by the operator's maintenance personnel. Work on drives, sensors, safety switches and on the electrical system is reserved for qualified specialists. The yearly service is carried out by Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG and documented in the portal.

1.3 Safety notes

The safety chapter of the operating instructions KT-500, chapter 2, applies to all work. Before any work that involves reaching into the machine, the line must be de-energised, secured against being switched on again and disconnected from the compressed air. Protective hoods are opened only when the machine is at a standstill; the interlock is never bypassed. The folding and closing stations contain crushing points that are covered by the hoods during operation. Protective gloves must be worn when handling the carton magazine and the leaflet feeder because carton edges are sharp. This maintenance schedule does not repeat the safety rules in full; it presupposes them.

Other agents, in particular solvents and abrasive pastes, are not permitted and can lead to loss of warranty.

3 Product infeed and carton detection

3.1 Product infeed daily

The product infeed is dry cleaned daily. Blister residues and dust are removed from the infeed chain and the pushers with a brush. The sensors of the product infeed are checked for dirt and wiped with RM-05 if necessary. The interlock of the infeed hood is checked: open the hood, the machine must stop, the operator panel must display the message.

3.2 Adjustment of the carton detection cell

The carton detection cell detects whether a carton has been erected correctly before the product is pushed in. It is adjusted manually after every carton quality change and whenever the operator panel reports repeated carton faults. The adjustment is carried out with the machine at a standstill and the hood of the carton feed open; the line must be de-energised for this work. The sensitivity of the cell is set with the adjustment screw on the sensor housing until the indicator lamp of the cell lights up with a reference carton in the detection position and goes out without a carton. The result is noted in the shift log.

  • Clean the carton detection cell with a cloth moistened with RM-05
  • Place the reference carton of the loaded format in the detection position
  • Turn the adjustment screw until the indicator lamp responds to the reference carton only
  • Note the result in the shift log; have the cell checked by a specialist if no stable setting is found

The adjustment is not stored in the format data set. After a format change with a different carton quality, the adjustment must be repeated. A tool-free calibration from the operator panel is planned for a later version of the machine software.

4 Carton magazine and carton feed

The carton magazine is dry cleaned daily; carton dust is removed from the magazine guides and the suction cups are checked for damage. Weekly, the suction cups are cleaned with RM-12 and the vacuum hoses are checked for leaks by listening with vacuum applied. Monthly, the magazine guides are checked for wear and lubricated with RM-31; worn suction cups are replaced (part 2210). The carton feed rotor is checked monthly for free movement.

5 Leaflet feeder

The leaflet feeder folds and inserts the leaflet into the carton. It is dry cleaned daily; paper dust is removed from the folding rollers with a brush. Weekly, the folding rollers are checked for paper residues and the leaflet detection sensor is wiped with RM-05. Quarterly, the folding rollers are checked for wear: a leaflet that is folded askew or jams indicates worn rollers, which are replaced in pairs (part 2320). The rollers are checked quarterly for wear and replaced in pairs when worn.

6 Folding and closing station

The folding and closing station is dry cleaned daily. Weekly, the guides are lubricated with RM-31 and the closing tongues are checked for wear. For machines with glue closing, the glue nozzles are cleaned weekly with a cloth moistened with RM-12 when the glue system is cold; the glue tank is cleaned monthly. Monthly, the folding station is checked for correct carton closure: a carton that opens again after closing indicates worn closing tongues (part 2410) or incorrectly set glue application. Every six months the specialist checks the drive of the closing station and the safety switches of the hoods.

7 Electrical system and safety devices

The safety switches of the protective hoods are checked daily by the function test according to chapter 3.1. Every six months the qualified specialist additionally checks the emergency stop and the connectors of the sensors. Yearly, as part of the service, the electrical installation is checked according to the wiring diagram of the machine. The results are recorded in the maintenance log. A safety device that fails the check leads to a standstill of the machine until it has been repaired.

9 Maintenance log

Every maintenance task is documented with date, serial number, task carried out, cleaning agent used, parts replaced and signature of the person carrying it out. The adjustment of the carton detection cell is documented with the carton quality used. The log can be kept in the myTannwald portal; there it is assigned to the serial number and can be viewed by the service of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG. The log is part of the machine documentation and must be presented at audits.

  1. Daily: dry cleaning of all stations, check of the interlock, check of suction cups and sensors.
  2. Weekly: clean suction cups, check folding rollers, lubricate guides, clean glue nozzles.
  3. Monthly: check magazine guides and rotor, check closing tongues, clean glue tank; quarterly check folding rollers for wear.
  4. Every six months: check vacuum pump, drive of the closing station, safety switches and emergency stop.
  5. Yearly: service by Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG with check of the electrical installation.

10 Notes on this version

Version 1.0 of this maintenance schedule is the first edition for the KT-500 series and was released in March 2025. It is based on the maintenance schedule TB-200 and on the service reports of the first KT-500 machines in the field. Feedback from operators on the manual adjustment of the carton detection cell and on the wear of the folding rollers with thin leaflet papers is collected by the service of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG and will be considered in the next version. The myTannwald portal shows the change history of this document and marks the current version for all machines of type KT-500.

Operators are asked to report deviations from the intervals of this schedule, in particular shortened intervals for dusty carton qualities, with a reason in the maintenance log. These reports are the basis for the revision of the intervals in the next version. Questions on the schedule can be asked to the assistant in the portal, which answers from the current version and cites the section.
