1 Training objective

This training document is intended for operators of the KT-500 cartoner who are newly assigned to the machine or who refresh their knowledge. It supplements the operating instructions KT-500 and does not replace them. After the training, participants know the design of the KT-500, the safety rules, the sequence of a shift from switching on to switching off, the refilling of cartons and leaflets, the calibration of the carton detection cell and the most frequent faults with their remedies. The training lasts one day and concludes with a practical exercise on the machine.

The KT-500 is usually operated behind a TB-200 or TB-400 blister line. The interaction of the machines in the line is the subject of the training document line operation; this document concentrates on the cartoner itself. Participants who will be responsible for a whole line attend the line operation training in addition.

2 Safety

The safety rules of the operating instructions KT-500, chapter 2, apply to the training and to later operation. The most important rules are repeated here because they are practised in the training. The training does not replace reading the safety chapter; every participant confirms before the practical exercise that they have read the chapter.

2.1 The five basic rules

  1. Before any work on a station, de-energise the line and secure it against being switched on again; disconnect the compressed air. In a coupled line, de-energise the blister line as well.
  2. Open protective hoods only when the machine is at a standstill; never bypass, block or remove the interlock.
  3. Do not reach into running stations, not even to free a jammed carton or a leaflet.
  4. Wear protective gloves when refilling cartons and leaflets; carton edges are sharp. Touch the glue system only when the operator panel shows the cooling value.
  5. Use only approved cleaning agents and original spare parts; keep unauthorised persons away from the working area.

2.2 Crushing points on the KT-500

The KT-500 has crushing points at the insertion fingers, at the suction cups of the carton magazine, at the folding rollers of the leaflet folder and at the folding tools of the closing station. During operation these points are covered by the interlocked hoods or by fixed guards. In the training, participants learn where the crushing points are and why a carton jam is removed only with the line de-energised: even in the stop state, drives can start when a fault is acknowledged by another person at the operator panel.

2.3 What is not shown in the training

The training does not show work on drives, sensors, safety switches, the glue system or the electrical system; this work is reserved for qualified specialists. It does not show test modes in which safety functions are disabled; such modes do not exist for the operator. Questions about them are answered by the instructor with a reference to the safety chapter.

3 Design of the KT-500

The KT-500 consists of the product infeed, the carton magazine with carton feed and detection cell, the leaflet folder, the insertion station and the folding and closing station. The following table summarises the stations with the task of the operator at each station.

The operator panel shows the operating state, the fill levels of carton magazine and leaflet folder, the output and the current messages. Faults are displayed with an error code following the pattern K-number; the codes are explained in the operating instructions KT-500, chapter 9, and are not repeated in this training document.

4 Sequence of a shift

4.1 Switching on and preparing

The shift begins with the visual inspection of all stations for carton residues, foreign objects and damage, followed by the check of the interlock on all protective hoods. Then the main switch and the compressed air are switched on and the operator panel is started. The format is selected, the carton magazine and the leaflet folder are filled. With a glue system, the heating time is awaited until the display is green. Idle mode with three cartons without product is run and the closure is checked. Only then is the blister line started.

4.2 Refilling cartons and leaflets

Carton magazine and leaflet folder are refilled from outside during operation; no hood is opened for this. The folding cartons are placed flat and with the opening side to the front into the magazine. A frequent error in practice is placing cartons the wrong way round: the carton feed then cannot erect them and the detection cell reports a carton that is not erected. Leaflets are placed as a stack into the folder, aligned to the stop. In the training, refilling is practised until the orientation is correct without thinking.

4.3 Calibration of the carton detection cell

The carton detection cell is calibrated weekly and after every carton quality change according to the maintenance schedule KT-500, section 3.2. The calibration is started from the maintenance menu of the operator panel with the machine at a standstill and the hood of the carton feed closed; the reference carton of the loaded format is placed in the calibration position. The operator panel reports the result. In the training, the calibration is practised with two carton qualities so that participants see how the cell reacts to a different carton surface. A failed calibration is repeated after cleaning the cell with RM-05; if it fails again, the specialist is called.

4.4 Production mode and switching off

In production mode the operator checks three cartons every hour for closure, completeness and position of the leaflet and records the result in the shift log. Rejected cartons are counted by the machine; an increase in rejects is reported to the line leader. At the end of the shift the blister line is stopped first, the KT-500 runs empty until all blisters are packed, then it is stopped in a controlled way and dry cleaned. With a glue system, the system is switched to standby according to the operator panel; the glue tank is not opened by the operator.

6 Cleaning in the training

The cleaning of the KT-500 follows the maintenance schedule KT-500. In the training, daily dry cleaning is practised: carton dust is removed from infeed chain, magazine, folding rollers and folding tools with the brush; sensors are wiped with a cloth moistened with RM-05, never sprayed. The weekly cleaning of the suction cups with RM-12 is shown. The cleaning of the glue system is explained but not carried out because it is reserved for the specialist and requires a cold system. The approved cleaning agents are RM-12, RM-05, RM-31 and, for the glue system, RM-40; other agents, in particular solvents, damage the suction cups and the plastic guides.

8 Practical exercise and assessment

The practical exercise comprises the check of the interlock on all hoods, the refilling of cartons and leaflets with correct orientation, the calibration of the carton detection cell with two carton qualities, idle mode with check of the closure and the removal of a staged carton jam with the line de-energised. The exercise is accompanied by the instructor and confirmed in the training record.

The assessment consists of ten questions on safety, design and sequence. Examples: What happens when a protective hood is opened during operation? Why is a carton jam removed only with the line de-energised? How is the carton detection cell calibrated? Which way round are the cartons placed in the magazine? Who may clean the glue system? The answers are given in this document and in the operating instructions KT-500. The training record is assigned to the person and the serial number in the myTannwald portal.
