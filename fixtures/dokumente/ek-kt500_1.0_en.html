1 Notes on the catalogue

The spare parts catalogue KT-500 contains all spare and wear parts of the KT-500 cartoner, arranged by station. Each part is listed with part number, designation, installation location and notes on replacement. The part numbers apply to all machines of type KT-500 from year of manufacture 2021 onwards; deviating parts for customer-specific carton formats are given in the machine documentation of the respective serial number.

Orders are placed via the support of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG, stating the serial number and the part number. The catalogue contains no information on prices, availability or delivery times; this information is provided by support on request. Only original spare parts are approved for the KT-500. The installation of third-party parts leads to loss of conformity and warranty.

1.1 Structure of the part numbers

The part numbers of the KT-500 have four digits and begin with the digit 2. The second digit designates the station: 1 for product infeed, 2 for carton magazine and carton feed, 3 for leaflet folder, 4 for insertion, folding and closing station, 6 for control system and electrical equipment, 7 for tools and general parts. Format-dependent parts additionally carry the format number of the carton format. For format-dependent parts, the format number must be stated when ordering.

1.2 Safety notes on part replacement

The safety chapter of the operating instructions KT-500, chapter 2, applies to every part replacement. The line must be de-energised and secured against being switched on again, and the compressed air must be disconnected; in a coupled line the blister line is de-energised as well. Drives, sensors, safety switches, the glue system and electrical parts may only be replaced by a qualified specialist. Hot parts of the glue system are touched only after cooling down. This catalogue does not describe work steps; it names the parts and refers to the operating instructions and the maintenance schedule KT-500.

8 Ordering, return and stocking

Orders contain serial number, part number, designation, quantity and, for format-dependent parts, the format number. Support confirms the order and states availability and delivery date; the catalogue itself gives no information on this. Defective parts replaced under warranty must be returned to Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG with the return note. Sensors and the carton detection cell are marked with the part number and the serial number of the machine before return.

Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG recommends keeping a basic stock of wear parts per machine at the site: four suction cups 2210, one vacuum hose set 2215, one folding roller pair 2320, one closing tongue set 2410 per format, one glue nozzle 2420 for machines with glue system and one brush set 2750. With this stock, the most frequent faults at the carton feed, the leaflet folder and the closure can be remedied without loss of production. Wear parts are stored dry, dust-free and in their original packaging; suction cups are kept in the closed bag until installation because the rubber ages when exposed to light.

The consumption of wear parts is recorded in the maintenance log. A conspicuously high consumption of suction cups indicates a carton quality with rough surface or an incorrectly set vacuum; a high consumption of folding rollers indicates thin leaflet papers. Both should be reported to the service of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG so that the intervals of the maintenance schedule can be adjusted in the next version.

9 Format-dependent parts

Format-dependent parts of the KT-500 are tailored to a specific carton format and carry the format number. Each carton format comprises the pushers and guide rails of the product infeed, the magazine guides, the folding pocket of the leaflet folder, the insertion fingers and insertion rail, the closing tongues and the folding tools. These parts are managed together as a format set and may only be combined within the same format set. A format set is delivered complete when a new carton format is ordered and is assigned to the serial number in the myTannwald portal.

Many format changes on the KT-500 are carried out by adjustment on scales rather than by replacing parts; the format data set states which parts must be replaced and which settings must be made. Damaged format parts, in particular closing tongues with broken edges and folding tools with deformed surfaces, are not reused but sent to Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG for repair or replaced. For customer-specific carton formats not included in the standard range, the part numbers from the machine documentation of the respective serial number apply.

10 Using the catalogue in the portal

In the myTannwald portal this catalogue is assigned to the serial number of your machine. The search in the portal finds part numbers directly: entering a four-digit part number leads to the table row with designation and installation location. The assistant answers questions on designation, installation location and replacement notes from this catalogue and cites the chapter. Questions on prices, availability and delivery times cannot be answered by the assistant from the catalogue; in this case it offers a support request. New versions of the catalogue are published in the portal with a change note, and the change history shows which part numbers have been added, replaced or deleted.

11 Revision history

Version 1.0 of this catalogue is the first edition for the KT-500 series and covers all machines from year of manufacture 2021 onwards. The part numbers were assigned on the basis of the production documentation and the service reports of the first machines in the field. Parts that have proven to be wear parts in the field, in particular the suction cups, the folding rollers and the closing tongues, are marked accordingly. Future versions will add the parts of the optional leaflet pre-folder and the part numbers of the second generation of the carton detection cell, which allows calibration from the operator panel. Operators are asked to report missing part numbers to support so that they can be added in the next version.
