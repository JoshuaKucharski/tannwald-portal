1 Declaration of conformity

Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG declares under its sole responsibility that the TB-200 blister line in the version from year of manufacture 2019 onwards complies with the essential health and safety requirements of the applicable regulations for machinery. This declaration applies to all machines of type TB-200 that are delivered in the configuration described in chapter 3 and becomes invalid in the event of changes to the machine that have not been approved by Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG.

The declaration is part of the documentation of every machine and is assigned to the serial number in the myTannwald portal. It is provided in German and English; in case of discrepancies the German version applies. The original of the declaration with signature is kept by Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG; a certified copy is stored in the portal.

1.2 Object of the declaration

The object of the declaration is the TB-200 blister line as a complete machine with unwinding station, forming station, filling station, sealing station and punching station, including the control system, the protective hoods with interlock, the emergency stop device and the two-hand control for jog mode. The high-frequency sealing option is the object of a supplementary declaration that is delivered with the option.

2 Safety

The conformity of the TB-200 is based on the protective measures listed in chapter 4. It applies only as long as these protective measures are unchanged and functional. The safety chapter of the operating instructions TB-200, chapter 2, describes the rules that the operator must observe for safe operation; this declaration does not replace the safety chapter.

The conformity expires in particular if an interlock is bypassed, blocked or removed, if a protective hood is replaced by a part that has not been approved, if the two-hand control is circumvented, if heating cartridges or safety switches are replaced by third-party parts or if the machine is operated outside the intended use described in the operating instructions. In these cases the operator is responsible for the safety of the machine.

3 Configuration and scope

The declaration applies to the TB-200 in the standard configuration and in the Pharma-GMP configuration. Both configurations use the same protective devices; the Pharma-GMP configuration adds the camera fill control and the batch documentation, which have no safety function. Customer-specific formats are covered by the declaration provided that the format parts are supplied by Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG and assigned to the serial number in the portal.

4 Protective measures

The following protective measures are part of the machine and the basis of the conformity. They are checked at the acceptance of every machine and documented in the acceptance report; the operator rechecks them regularly according to the maintenance schedule TB-200.

  • Interlocked protective hoods at forming, sealing and punching station; opening a hood stops the machine.
  • Emergency stop device at the operator panel and at the rear; the emergency stop stops all movements and switches off the heaters.
  • Two-hand control for jog mode during set-up.
  • Main switch with the possibility of securing it against being switched on again.
  • Fixed guards at rollers and drives, removable only with tools.
  • Temperature monitoring of forming and sealing station with shutdown on sensor fault.
  • Marking of hot surfaces and crushing points on the machine.

The effectiveness of the protective measures has been demonstrated by a risk assessment that is part of the technical file. The technical file is kept by Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG and is made available to the competent authorities on reasoned request.

5 Applied basis

The conformity was assessed on the basis of the applicable regulations for machinery, for electrical equipment and for electromagnetic compatibility as well as the relevant harmonised standards for packaging machines, for safety-related control systems and for interlocking devices. The complete list of the applied standards with date of issue is part of the technical file and is not repeated in this certificate because it is updated when standards change; the operator receives the currently valid list on request.

6 Obligations of the operator

The operator maintains the protective measures according to chapter 4 and checks them according to the maintenance schedule TB-200. The operator employs only trained personnel, keeps the operating instructions available at the installation site and documents maintenance and part replacement in the maintenance log. Changes to the machine that affect the protective measures are agreed with Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG in advance. If the machine is passed on, the operator hands over this declaration together with the operating instructions.

Safety bulletins that Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG publishes in the myTannwald portal supplement this declaration. They describe measures that are required to maintain conformity, such as the check of the protective hood of the sealing station according to safety bulletin SB-2026-07 or the replacement of heating cartridges according to safety bulletin SB-2026-08. The operator confirms in the portal that safety bulletins with mandatory confirmation have been read; the confirmation is recorded per user and serial number.

7 Validity and changes

This declaration is valid without time limit for machines that are delivered in the described configuration and operated according to the operating instructions. It is reissued when the configuration of the series, the applied basis or the protective measures change. New versions are published in the myTannwald portal with a change note; the change history shows which chapters have been changed. For machines with the high-frequency sealing option, the supplementary declaration assigned to the serial number applies in addition.

9 Terms

The terms used in this certificate correspond to the operating instructions TB-200 and the maintenance schedule TB-200. The interlock is the safety switch on a protective hood that stops the machine when the hood is opened. The two-hand control is the device that permits movement in jog mode only when both buttons are pressed at the same time. Jog mode is the operating mode for set-up with open hoods in which the machine is moved only step by step and only while the two-hand control is actuated. The intended use is the operation described in the operating instructions for packaging solid dosage forms and small medical devices in blisters made of the films named in the film specification.

Not intended are in particular the packaging of other products without approval by Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG, operation with films outside the film specification, operation with open or removed protective hoods outside jog mode and any change to the control system, safety chain or heating circuits by unauthorised persons. Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG accepts no liability for damage resulting from use that is not intended; in these cases the conformity expires according to chapter 2.
