1 Design and function

The KT-500 cartoner is a horizontal packaging machine that packs blisters from an upstream TB-200 or TB-400 blister line together with a folded leaflet into folding cartons. It erects the folding carton from the magazine, pushes blister and leaflet in and closes the carton by tucking or gluing. These operating instructions apply to all machines of type KT-500 from year of manufacture 2021 onwards and describe the intended operation by trained personnel.

The instructions are part of the machine and must be kept within easy reach at the installation site. Changes compared with the previous version are described in the change note in the myTannwald portal. For operation in a line with a blister line, the training document line operation, which describes the interaction of the machines, applies in addition.

1.1 Overview of the stations

The KT-500 consists of five stations. The product infeed takes the blisters from the outfeed belt of the blister line and groups them according to pack size. The carton magazine stores the flat folding cartons and erects them with suction cups. The detection cell checks whether the carton has been erected correctly. The leaflet folder folds the leaflet and feeds it to the insertion. The folding and closing station closes the carton and transfers it to the outfeed belt.

  • Product infeed with infeed chain, pushers and grouping
  • Carton magazine with suction cups, rotor and detection cell
  • Leaflet folder with folding rollers and leaflet detection
  • Insertion station with insertion fingers
  • Folding and closing station with tuck closure or glue system, outfeed belt

2 Safety

This chapter contains the safety rules for all work on the KT-500. Read it completely before you switch on the machine for the first time. The assistant in the myTannwald portal refers to this chapter for safety-related questions and does not give shortened instructions.

2.1 Basic rules

The machine may only be operated by trained persons. Work on drives, on the electrical system, on the pneumatics and on the glue system is reserved for qualified specialists. Unauthorised persons, in particular children and unaccompanied visitors, must not stay in the working area.

  • Before any work on a station, de-energise the line, secure it against being switched on again and disconnect the compressed air.
  • Open protective hoods only when the machine is at a standstill. Never bypass, block or remove the interlock.
  • Do not reach into running stations, not even to remove a jammed carton.
  • Wear protective gloves when refilling the carton magazine and the leaflet folder; carton edges are sharp.
  • Use only approved cleaning agents and original spare parts.

2.2 Protective hoods and interlock

The protective hoods of the product infeed, the insertion station and the folding and closing station are interlocked by safety switches. Opening a hood stops the machine and, in a coupled line, also the upstream blister line. The carton magazine and the leaflet folder can be refilled from outside without opening a hood; their ranges of movement are secured by fixed guards. Operation with a bypassed, blocked or removed interlock is prohibited. The interlock is checked every day before the start of production.

2.3 De-energising the line

Before cleaning, maintenance, format change and troubleshooting that involves reaching into the machine, the line must be de-energised at the main switch and secured with a padlock against being switched on again. In a coupled line, the blister line must also be de-energised because its outfeed belt extends into the product infeed of the KT-500. The compressed air must be disconnected and the residual pressure released. The operator's lockout procedure defines the exact sequence.

2.4 Glue system and hot surfaces

On machines with a glue system, glue tank and nozzles become hot during operation. Touch these parts only when the operator panel shows the cooling value of the glue system. The glue tank is filled and cleaned only when the system is cold and only by a qualified specialist. Hot glue causes burns; spattered glue is removed only after it has cooled down.

2.5 Crushing points and carton jam

Crushing points exist at the insertion fingers, at the suction cups of the carton magazine, at the folding rollers of the leaflet folder and at the folding tools of the closing station. A carton jam is removed only with the line de-energised. Never reach into the insertion to free a jammed carton while the machine is switched on; even in the stop state, drives can start when a fault is acknowledged.

3 Transport and installation

The KT-500 is delivered on a transport pallet and transported with an industrial truck at the marked lifting points. The installation site must be level and load-bearing. When installed in a line, the product infeed is aligned to the height of the outfeed belt of the blister line; the transfer is checked in idle mode with empty blisters. The electrical connection, the compressed air connection and the coupling to the blister line are made by the qualified specialist according to the wiring diagram KT-500.

4 Commissioning

Initial commissioning is carried out by a service technician of Tannwald Verpackungstechnik AG. The following sequence applies to daily start-up:

  1. Visual inspection of all stations for carton residues, foreign objects and damage.
  2. Check of the interlock on all protective hoods according to chapter 2.2.
  3. Switch on the main switch, open the compressed air, start the operator panel.
  4. Select the format, fill the carton magazine and the leaflet folder.
  5. With a glue system, wait for the heating time until the display is green.
  6. Run idle mode with three cartons without product and check the closure.

5 Operation

5.1 Operator panel

The operator panel shows the operating state, the fill levels of carton magazine and leaflet folder, the output and the current messages. Faults are displayed with an error code following the pattern K-number; the meaning of the codes is given in chapter 9. In a coupled line, the KT-500 takes start and stop from the blister line and reports its readiness back.

5.2 Refilling cartons and leaflets

Carton magazine and leaflet folder are refilled from outside during operation. The folding cartons are placed flat and with the opening side to the front into the magazine; the fill level display on the operator panel warns before the magazine runs empty. Leaflets are placed as a stack into the folder; pre-folded leaflets are placed into the leaflet infeed. Protective gloves must be worn when refilling.

5.3 Production mode

In production mode the control system monitors the transfer of the blisters, the detection of the erected carton, the leaflet detection and the closure. A carton without product or without leaflet is rejected and counted. The operator checks three cartons every hour for closure, completeness and position of the leaflet.

6 Format change

A format change comprises the adjustment of the carton magazine, the insertion fingers, the folding tools and the product infeed to the new carton size as well as loading of the format data set. Adjustments that involve reaching into the machine are carried out with the line de-energised. The setting values can be read on scales and are stored in the format data set. After the format change, the detection cell must be calibrated according to the maintenance schedule KT-500 if the carton quality has changed.

7 Cleaning and maintenance

The maintenance schedule KT-500 is the binding document for intervals, cleaning agents and parts. The machine is dry cleaned daily: carton dust is removed from infeed chain, magazine, folding rollers and folding tools with the brush. Sensors are wiped with a cloth moistened with RM-05. Weekly, the suction cups are cleaned and the detection cell is calibrated. The glue system is cleaned only when the system is cold and only by a qualified specialist. Chapter 2.3 applies to all work that involves reaching into the machine.

8 Spare parts

Spare parts are listed in the spare parts catalogue KT-500 with part number, designation and installation location. Use only original spare parts. For orders and questions about availability, contact support; the instructions contain no information on delivery times or prices.

9.2 Faults at the detection cell

If the detection cell repeatedly reports a carton that is not erected although the carton is visibly erected, the cell is dirty or not calibrated to the carton quality. Clean the cell with a cloth moistened with RM-05 and calibrate it according to the maintenance schedule KT-500, section 3.2. If the message remains after calibration, the cell must be checked by a specialist. A cell that does not detect erected cartons must not be bypassed, because otherwise product is pushed into cartons that are not erected and the insertion station becomes blocked.

9.3 Faults at the closure

If a carton opens again after closing, with tuck closure the folding tools or the closing tongues are worn or not adjusted to the format. With a glue system, the glue application is too low or the system is not at temperature. Check the format setting of the folding tools; the glue application is adjusted by a qualified specialist. Cartons with an open closure are rejected and counted; an increase in rejects is an early sign of wear.

10 Decommissioning and disposal

For a longer standstill the machine is de-energised, disconnected from the compressed air, cleaned and covered against dust. With a glue system, the glue tank is emptied and cleaned by a qualified specialist. On final decommissioning, electronics, metals and plastics must be separated and sent for recycling. The operator documents the decommissioning in the myTannwald portal.

11 Operation in the line

The KT-500 is usually operated directly behind a TB-200 or TB-400 blister line. The coupling transmits start, stop and readiness between the machines: if the blister line stops, the KT-500 runs empty and waits; if the KT-500 stops, the blister line reports the fault transfer to cartoner disturbed and stops after the outfeed belt has filled up. With a TB-400 with two webs, the product infeed takes the blisters of both webs from the common outfeed belt and groups them according to pack size. The training document line operation describes the procedures for starting up and shutting down the whole line as well as the responsibilities when several persons work on one line.
